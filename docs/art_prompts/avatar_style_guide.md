# Avatar Style Guide

Style version: `teen_modular_v1`

Design avatars for high-school students ages 15-18. The look should feel modern, readable, and collectible: streetwear, varsity, esports, and study-tech cues are preferred. Avoid preschool proportions, baby styling, tiny full-body characters, or heavily detailed fashion illustration.

Use a transparent 512x512 PNG canvas. Keep the character centered with a consistent waist-up or upper-thigh crop. The face must stay readable at small icon sizes, the head area must leave room for hats, and the torso must support modular overlays such as chains, badges, scarves, jackets, straps, auras, and side-slot companions.

Accessory zones:
- Headwear: simplified hair volume, no extreme hair shapes.
- Eyewear and face accessories: clear facial area with eyes and nose unobstructed in the base.
- Neck and chest: clean hoodie, jacket, or top silhouette.
- Back/aura/frame: centered behind the avatar.
- Side slots: independent pets or floating props around the avatar, never handheld.

Rendering rules:
- Transparent background.
- Clean semi-flat cartoon shapes with light cel shading.
- Consistent head position and torso scale.
- No full scene backgrounds.
- No true handheld overlays unless a future hand-mask system is added.
